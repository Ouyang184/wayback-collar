How it works

  1. Pair it with your phone

    Add the collar in Apple Find My on an iPhone or Google Find Hub on Android. It takes about a minute.

  2. Your dog wears it like any collar

    There is no charging cable. The tag runs on a coin battery you can replace.

  3. If your dog gets out, open the map

    Each time a phone on the same network passes close by, the location updates on your map.

What it can't do

It is not GPS

The tag uses Bluetooth and other people's phones. In town it updates often. On a trail or a farm with nobody around, it may not update at all.

There is no live trail

You see the last place a phone picked up the tag. You do not see a dot moving in real time.

It is not for every dog

For a dog that runs for miles in open country, a GPS collar with a monthly plan is the better tool.
